# LMU track + car roster (facts, cited)

Snapshot as of research date 2026-08-27/28. LMU adds content via paid DLC
packs and free updates, so re-verify before relying on this for a release.

## Tracks (14 total: 7 base game + 7 across paid DLC)

Base game:
- Algarve International Circuit (Portimão)
- Bahrain International Circuit
- Circuit de la Sarthe (Le Mans)
- Fuji International Speedway
- Monza
- Sebring
- Spa-Francorchamps

2024 content packs:
- Imola
- Circuit of the Americas (COTA)
- Interlagos
- Lusail International Circuit (Qatar)

ELMS pack:
- Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya
- Circuit Paul Ricard
- Silverstone

US Track Pass:
- Laguna Seca
- Daytona International Speedway
- (additional tracks announced but TBC at research time)

Track lengths in km and per-track corner counts were **not** available from
the sources checked below — pull those from LMU's own track-select screen or
official specs when adding a new track file; do not estimate them here.

## Confidence / gaps
- Track/car lists confirmed via two independent secondary sources
  (traxion.gg articles) as of the dates below; primary confirmation is
  LMU's own in-game content list, not checked here.
- Exact model-year trims (e.g. which cars have both "base" and "Evo 2026"
  variants) are approximate — verify per-car before writing a car note.
