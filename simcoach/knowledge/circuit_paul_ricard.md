# Circuit Paul Ricard notes

5.842 km, 15 turns, WEC layout. A mix of flowing chicanes and long,
high-commitment sweepers (Courbe de Signes, Double Droite du Beausset) --
precision through the fast corners matters most since mistakes there are
described as especially costly.

## General
- [LMU] Rear tyres are called out as degrading quickly through the long
  loaded right-handers (notably the Beausset double-right).
- [LMU] Hypercar differs mainly in braking earlier/harder and being more
  sensitive to aggressive kerb use unsettling the aero platform.

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1-2 S de la Verrerie (right then left):** Medium-speed opening
  chicane, brake after the end of the side road, smooth direction change,
  stay central after T1; the right-hand section can be flat out if set up
  correctly.
- **T3-4 Virage de l'Hotel (right, then left):** T3 is a downhill
  right-hander, brake around 60 m, trail-brake to a late apex; T4 is a
  low-speed left prioritizing balance over entry speed, tight inside line
  to set up T5.
- **T5 Virage du Camp (right):** Low-speed right, smooth steering, very
  late apex for the best exit.
- **T6 Virage de la Ste Baume (right):** Lift for rotation, early throttle
  to prepare for the Mistral straight.
- **T7 (left):** Left bend right before the Mistral straight, use the full
  track, flat out.
- **T8-9 Chicane Nord (left then right):** Heavy straight-line braking
  around 70 m, trail-brake and cut the apex at T8, release the brakes to
  stabilize and cut the apex at T9, early throttle after.
- **T10 Courbe de Signes (right):** Very high-speed right, flat out for
  most GT3s, late turn-in and apex, maybe a tiny lift if tyres are cold --
  precision is critical, mistakes are costly.
- **T11 Double Droite du Beausset (right-right):** Long, loaded double
  right, trail-brake into the first apex, use the full width without
  getting too tight for the second apex, constant throttle balance --
  overheats/degrades the rears if over-rotated.
- **T12 Virage de Bendor (left):** Medium-speed left, deep trail-braking,
  early-to-late apex, full-width exit.
- **T13 Courbe du Garlaban (right):** Flat out, minimal steering to cut
  drag.
- **T14 Virage du Lac (left):** Slow left, heavy trail-braking, late apex,
  short-shift if traction-limited, keep the car left for T15.
- **T15 Virage du Pont (right):** Final corner onto the start/finish
  straight, medium braking, prioritize exit speed, full track width with
  an inside-kerb cut, watch track limits on exit.

## Common mistakes
Overdriving the Chicane Nord entries, excessive steering angle at Beausset,
early throttle at Virage du Lac, giving away exit speed from Ste Baume.
GT3-specific: cooking the rears in the long right-handers. Hypercar-
specific: aggressive throttle breaking the aero balance, breaking rhythm in
medium-speed corners.
