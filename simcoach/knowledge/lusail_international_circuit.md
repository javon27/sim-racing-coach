# Lusail International Circuit (Qatar) notes

5.419 km, 16 turns (6 left / 10 right per the wiki infobox), WEC layout. A
flowing, mostly high-speed circuit built from long U-shaped corners and
fast connecting bends -- the guide gives detailed braking-marker references
for essentially every corner.

## General
- [LMU] Several corners (T4-5, T8-9, T11) form connected sequences where
  the exit of one sets up the entry of the next -- treat them as one
  rhythm section rather than isolated corners.
- [LMU] Hypercar brakes noticeably earlier than GT3 into the two hairpins
  (T1, T10) to manage rear stability under hybrid torque.

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1 (right):** Long U-shaped hairpin, heavy braking from top speed
  around the 100m board, downshift to 2nd, turn-in about one car length
  after the 50m board, early-to-mid apex tight to the inside kerb,
  progressive throttle once rotation is complete.
- **T2 (left):** Long U-shaped left, position far right on entry, light
  braking at the red/white kerb, turn while braking (aero assists
  rotation), mid-to-late apex with trail-brake to the inside kerb.
- **T3 (right):** Fast curved right, flat out in GT3, inside kerb shortens
  the corner, smooth steering to protect the fronts.
- **T4 (right):** Medium-speed right, brake at the 3rd marker on the left,
  trail-brake turn-in, use as much inside kerb as possible, patient
  throttle.
- **T5 (right):** Continuation of the T4 sequence, usually just a lift,
  mid-to-late apex, wide exit carrying speed, move right immediately after.
- **T6 (left):** Slow hairpin, 1st/2nd gear, enter from far right, brake at
  or just after the blue Goodyear board, deep trail-brake to a mid-apex,
  early but smooth throttle.
- **T7 (right):** Long U-shaped medium-speed right, enter from far left,
  brake/turn at the 2nd Bosch board, very late apex, wait for rotation
  before throttle.
- **T8 (left):** Fast flat-out left, stay on the inside kerb throughout,
  sets up T9.
- **T9 (right):** Fast right with a short apex, entry from the left, small
  lift or brake dab, use the entire inside kerb, early throttle once off
  the kerb.
- **T10 (left):** Open, slow hairpin, heavy braking from far right, turn-in
  where the blue paint begins, 2nd gear, mid-apex focus, wide exit to the
  white line.
- **T11 (left):** Fast flat-out left, inside kerb the whole way through,
  minimal steering input.
- **T12 (right):** Fast right, enter from the left, turn-in where blue
  paint changes to orange, light braking if needed, mid-apex, high entry
  speed carried through.
- **T13 (right):** Fast right, lift or small brake, shorter apex, inside
  kerb carries the car to the exit kerb.
- **T14 (right):** Slowest of the three closing right-handers, lift or
  light brake, patience required, exit is narrower than it looks -- avoid
  the gravel.
- **T15 (left):** Medium-speed left, 3rd gear in GT3, light braking at the
  Michelin board, late apex, smooth throttle, let the car drift naturally
  to the exit kerb.
- **T16 (right):** Long medium-speed right similar to T1, critical exit
  onto the front straight, enter from the left with tyres on the kerb,
  brake just before the last Michelin board, trail-brake until rotation.

## Common mistakes
Overdriving the T1 entry, cooking tyres in the long right-handers, too
aggressive throttle at T7/T10, running wide at T14, fighting the car
instead of letting it flow. Hypercar adds: overheating the fronts in the
long rights, braking too aggressively into T1/T10.
