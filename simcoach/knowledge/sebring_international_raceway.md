# Sebring International Raceway notes

6.019 km, 17 turns, WEC layout. Famously bumpy former airfield surface --
several corners are explicitly called out as extremely bumpy on exit, so
much of the guide is about surviving the bumps rather than pure line
technique; braking references are mostly feel-based rather than visual.

## General
- [LMU] The bumpy surface repeatedly costs traction on exit (T1, T5, T10,
  T13, T17) -- progressive throttle and short-shifting are recurring themes.
- [LMU] Hypercar can generally brake later and trail-brake more aggressively
  than GT3 across the lap, but risks rear instability over the bumps if
  throttle is applied too early.

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1 (right):** No clear braking board -- brake ~2 car lengths before the
  right-side wall ends, mostly straight-line braking, trail-brake
  progressively, very late apex (reference: the "0" sign on the inside
  barrier), avoid the grass/exit kerb (bumpy, track-limits sensitive).
- **T2 (right):** Flat-out slight kink, positions the car right for T3.
- **T3 (left, direction not explicit but implied by flow):** Brake between
  the 300-200m boards, hard straight-line braking to ~100m, trail-brake to
  a very late apex on the dark asphalt patch, avoid the inside kerb.
- **T4 (right):** Short right-hander, stay close to the inside kerb without
  climbing it, position far right for T5.
- **T5:** Light braking (5-20%), rotation mostly via engine braking
  (downshift to 1st/2nd), late apex, short-shift to avoid wheelspin/TC,
  avoid the inside kerb, watch the bumpy high exit kerb.
- **T6 Big Bend (right):** Long flat-out right, stay close to the inside
  kerb, heavy traffic zone, smooth steering to protect tyres.
- **T7:** Prime overtaking spot -- brake from the far left between the
  400-300m boards, hard straight-line braking to ~100m, trail-brake to a
  late apex, don't climb the yellow sausage kerb, moderate exit-kerb use.
- **T8-9 Fangio (T8 slight right, T9 slight left):** Both flat out normally,
  take the shortest smooth line, avoid abrupt steering -- heavy multi-class
  traffic zone.
- **T10:** Important overtaking/defending zone -- brake just before the
  300m board, hard braking to the blue/white kerb, trail-brake to a late
  apex, extremely bumpy exit.
- **T11-12 (T11 long left, T12 slight right):** Both flat out if the T10
  exit is right; avoid running wide at the T11 exit, stay left to set up
  T13 braking.
- **T13 Tower:** Exit speed is critical for the following straight; no
  strong visual brake reference (feel-based, roughly parallel with the left
  kerb), immediate trail-brake, late apex, extremely bumpy exit.
- **T14 Bishop (left):** Flat-out left kink, mainly a traffic-awareness
  corner.
- **T15 The "S" (right then left):** High-speed overtaking zone, brake
  between the end of the left kerb and the 300m board; first leg (right) is
  hard-braked with an aggressive inside-kerb climb, second leg (left) is a
  light-throttle flowing transition.
- **T16 Le Mans (right, not explicit but named):** One of the most
  important corners -- braking reference is the end of the T15 exit kerb,
  aggressive inside-kerb use is fine, wide entry angle is okay, minimal
  trail-brake, progressive release into immediate throttle; applying
  throttle before full rotation is the main risk.
- **T17 Sunset Bend (right, not explicit but named):** Iconic bumpy final
  corner, double-apex line, brake just after the 100m line crossing, short
  firm braking, second apex near the Mobil signs with a major bump right
  there, short-shift on exit, strong overtaking zone.
