# Autodromo Jose Carlos Pace (Interlagos) notes

4.309 km, 15 turns, WEC layout. A short, technical "rhythm track" with
constant elevation change and short straights -- lap time comes from
smooth, consistent exits rather than raw entry speed, and tyres (front and
rear) wear quickly if corners are overdriven.

## General
- [LMU] Overdriving downhill corners (Mergulho, Descida do Lago) destabilizes
  the rear.
- [LMU] Brake earlier than instinct suggests on uphill sections to avoid
  lock-ups.
- [LMU] Hypercar guide adds that early throttle on uphill corners overloads
  the rear tyres and costs traction.

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1-2 Senna S:** Heavy braking into T1 (left), late apex, position left
  for T2 (mid-apex, full throttle).
- **T3 Curva do Sol:** High-speed downhill right, flat out normally, avoid
  aggressive kerb use; lift slightly if understeering.
- **T4 Descida do Lago:** Downhill left, brake after the sideroad on the
  right, light inside kerb use for rotation, throttle on exit.
- **T5 Long left:** Downhill left, medium brake, short-shift if
  traction-limited -- early exit matters for the front straight.
- **T6-7 Ferradura:** T6 is a fast right kink (mostly a lift, stay central
  for T7); T7 is a steep uphill right, late apex, throttle after the apex.
- **T8 Laranja:** Low-speed left, trail-brake to the apex, sparing inside
  kerb use, focus on rotation and exit.
- **T9 Pinheirinho:** Deep trail-brake, early smooth throttle after the
  apex, use the full track, gear 1-2 depending on the car.
- **T10 Bico de Pato:** Tight right hairpin, heavy braking with trail-brake
  rotation, late apex, enter wide and exit toward the middle of the track.
- **T11 Mergulho:** Fast downhill left, flat out, lift gently only if
  understeering.
- **T12 Juncao:** Medium-speed left, late apex, use full track width.
- **T13 Left bend / T14 Subida dos Boxes / T15 Main-straight kink:** All
  slight left-hand kinks -- minimal steering and the inside line to reduce
  drag and keep the shortest path onto the front straight.

## Common mistakes
Overdriving downhill corners (especially Mergulho and Descida do Lago), late
braking into hairpins stressing tyres, jerky steering in medium-speed kinks
killing exit speed, early throttle on uphill corners, excessive kerb use on
fast corners.
