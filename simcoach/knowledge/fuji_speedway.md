# Fuji Speedway notes

4.563 km, 16 turns, WEC layout. A mix of a long flat-out straight-into-heavy-
braking Turn 1, high-speed sweepers (100R, 300R), and a technical final
sector -- the wiki frames it around exit traction and aero stability rather
than outright entry speed.

## General
- [LMU] Turn 1 punishes deep trail-braking -- GT3 loses stability easily
  there; Hypercar instead uses full ABS with minimal steering to rotate.
- [LMU] Aggressive throttle at the hairpins overheats the rear tyres quickly
  in both classes.

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1 (unnamed, direction not stated):** Brake early and straight, avoid
  deep trail-braking, prioritize exit traction.
- **T3 (left):** Brake at 80 m, clip the apex, use full width and kerb,
  then set up centrally for 100R.
- **T4-5 100R (right):** Early turn-in causes persistent understeer -- hold
  a late apex, delay throttle, lift shortly after the side road on the
  right; smooth steering protects front tyre life.
- **T6-7 Hairpin (left):** Straight-line braking only, rotate before
  applying throttle; aggressive throttle overheats the rears fast.
- **T8-9 300R (right):** Stay flat out with minimal steering to cut drag.
- **T10-12 Dunlop Corner/Chicane (direction not stated):** Aggressive inside
  kerb possible on T10-11 (can unsettle the car), early throttle out of
  T11; cut the inside kerb a little at T12, stay flat out, and position
  left for sector 3.
- **T13 (right):** Requires commitment and steady steering -- corrections
  cost real time; mid-to-late exit, don't run wide.
- **T14-15 (left):** Brake diagonally from the left of T14 to the right of
  T15, prioritize the entry, early-to-mid apex, position left for T16.
- **T16 Panasonic Corner (direction not stated):** Brake straight on the
  left side of the track, use flat kerbs, late apex -- exit speed is the
  priority.

## Setup baselines
**GT3:** medium rear wing (traction-focused), higher differential preload,
brake cooling slightly open, traction control one step above baseline.

**Hypercar:** medium-high rear wing (aero stability), moderate differential
preload, brake cooling opened as required, traction control two steps above
baseline.

## Common mistakes
Overdriving the Turn 1 entry, throttle too early at the hairpins, excessive
kerb use in the final sector (also loses downforce for Hypercar).
