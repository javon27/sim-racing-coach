# Circuit of the Americas (COTA) notes

5.513 km, 20 turns, WEC layout. Built around a steep uphill Turn 1, a long
flowing Esses section (T3-7), a slow "Bobby Pin" hairpin, and a multi-apex
Stadium section (T16-18) -- the guide stresses car balance through the Esses
and Stadium sequences over outright pace.

## General
- [LMU] The Esses (T3-7) and Stadium section (T16-19) are both described as
  rhythm sequences where corrections between corners cost more than any
  single apex.
- [LMU] Hypercar repeatedly needs to avoid front lock on the steep Turn 1
  crest and manage hybrid torque snap on exits (T9, T11).

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1 Big Red (direction not stated):** Very steep uphill braking zone,
  brake at the second board on the right, stay fully straight under
  braking, late apex, avoid the inside kerb (destabilizes the rear), let
  the car drift wide naturally.
- **T2 Right Bend (right):** Flat out, use the whole track, set up
  middle/right for T3.
- **T3 Esses entry (left):** Short kink, slight brake, minimal steering,
  cut the inside kerb, position left for T4.
- **T4 Esses (right):** Medium-speed, lift or slight brake, full width,
  cut the inside kerb.
- **T5 Esses (left):** Continuous direction change, no hard braking, cut
  the inside kerb, smooth transition, balanced throttle to avoid a rear
  snap.
- **T6 Esses (right):** Long bend, commit to the rhythm, slight brake and
  kerb use for rotation.
- **T7 Esses (left):** Final part of the high-speed Esses, slight brake,
  full width, avoid the sausage kerb.
- **T8 Medium Right (right):** Short braking, focus on exit positioning,
  keep the exit tight to the right.
- **T9 Tight Left (left):** Heavy braking, trail-brake rotation, avoid the
  inside kerb (destabilizes the rear), short-shift if traction is weak.
- **T10 Tiny Bend (left):** Tiny flat-out left, use the whole track to
  reduce drag.
- **T11 The Bobby Pin (direction not stated):** One of the slowest corners,
  brake straight, very late apex, running wide is acceptable, don't rush
  the throttle -- Hypercar guide warns hybrid torque can snap the rear here.
- **T12 Hairpin Left (left):** Heavy braking downhill into a flat corner,
  brake at the second board on the right, trail-brake helps rotation, avoid
  a too-deep entry that compromises the exit.
- **T13 Slow Right (right):** Straight-line braking only, cut the inside
  kerb, early throttle risks understeer.
- **T14 Medium Right (right):** Short transition corner, normally flat out,
  position right for T15.
- **T15 Medium Double Left (left):** Double-apex, maintain balance, avoid
  over-rotation, brake only in a straight line, early first apex, late
  second apex.
- **T16-18 Stadium section (all right):** Conservative braking with an
  early apex and early turn-in at T16; short lift and patient throttle at
  T17; continuous mid-speed flow at T18 where balance matters more than
  outright speed.
- **T19 Left Corner (left):** Fast, late apex, slight inside-kerb cut,
  early throttle only if the steering is straight.
- **T20 The Andretti (direction not stated):** Last corner onto the
  start/finish straight, medium braking, prioritize exit speed, watch the
  exit-kerb track limits.

## Common mistakes
Overdriving the T1 entry, cooking the rears in T9/T11, too aggressive
through the Esses, excessive kerb use in the Stadium section, throttle
before the car is settled.
