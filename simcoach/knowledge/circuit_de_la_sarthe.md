# Circuit de la Sarthe (Le Mans) notes

13.626 km, 38 turns, WEC layout (the corner-map JSON `simcoach/tracks/
lemans.json` already exists and was not touched per project instructions --
this note adds the wiki's driving detail alongside it). By far the longest
track in the roster; the guide repeatedly warns that losing focus over the
lap length is itself a common mistake.

## General
- [LMU] ABS activation is a recurring risk in GT3 through the Dunlop and
  Ford chicanes -- brake early and straight rather than trail-braking hard
  into them.
- [LMU] Hypercar's main differentiator is hybrid-torque wheelspin risk on
  exits (Tertre Rouge, both Mulsanne chicanes) rather than different lines.
- [LMU] Setup baseline, GT3: low rear wing, brake cooling open-to-slightly-
  closed, slightly higher ride height, stable-on-throttle differential,
  conservative traction control.
- [LMU] Setup baseline, Hypercar: low-drag aero with a stable rear
  platform, moderately open brake cooling, slightly higher ride height,
  stable-on-throttle differential, energy deployment optimized across the
  three long straights.

## Corner-by-corner
- **T1 Dunlop Curve:** Use the track and kerb on the left for entry, late
  apex staying close to the right, light lift if needed.
- **T2-3 Dunlop Chicane:** Heavy uphill braking, brake early and straight
  (ABS intervention is common in GT3 -- don't brake too deep into it), use
  the left kerb for rotation instead of aggressive trail-braking; on the
  right-hand exit, straighten the car before throttle and use all the exit
  kerb (track limits).
- **T4-5 Esses de la Foret:** A high-speed right (flat out, tight to the
  right on exit), a medium-speed left with smooth steering, then a
  right-hand continuation that balances the car before throttle to set up
  Tertre Rouge -- don't run wide.
- **T6 Tertre Rouge:** Critical right-hander onto the Mulsanne straight,
  light braking, late apex, cut the inside kerb, traction is everything
  (no wheelspin) -- a poor exit costs seconds on the straight.
- **T7 First Mulsanne Chicane:** One of the hardest braking zones, brake
  early and straight between 100-75 m, careful downshifting to avoid rear
  instability, inside kerbs on entry; exit is a left-right sequence
  prioritizing traction over aggression.
- **T8 Second Mulsanne Chicane:** Similar braking around 75 m with a slight
  camber change, conservative brakes, slight kerb cut; on exit, straighten
  the car and use the full width before applying throttle past the
  right-hander's apex.
- **T9 Mulsanne Corner:** Tight right-hander, brake early, rotate first and
  throttle second -- rear tyres overheat easily here.
- **T10 Indianapolis entry:** High-speed kink taken under braking, brake in
  a straight line before turn-in, stability is critical.
- **T11 Indianapolis apex/exit:** Left-hand corner, late apex, don't
  over-rotate; the downhill exit needs gentle throttle (rear instability is
  common in GT3).
- **T12 Arnage:** The slowest corner on the circuit, heavy braking at the
  second board, very late apex, don't run wide on exit, short-shift if
  traction is poor -- the exit defines the next straight.
- **T13 No Name Corner:** A minor bend, not important, don't overuse
  steering.
- **T14-16 Porsche Curves:** A fast right with a light lift; a high-speed
  double-left where aero balance matters and running wide means hitting the
  wall (mid-apex first, late apex second, keep tight left after); a long
  right-hand continuation using the full width, lifting through the first
  half and reapplying throttle in the second half, tight right at the end
  to open up Karting.
- **T17 Karting:** Fast left, just a light brake tap, maintain rhythm --
  any correction costs speed, inside kerbs are cuttable.
- **T18-19 Ford Chicane 1:** Heavy braking at the start of the kerb, use
  the full track to the right, cut the kerbs on entry with straight
  steering (ABS activation likely); stay right afterward to set up Ford
  Chicane 2.
- **T20-21 Ford Chicane 2:** The slowest chicane on the track -- cut the
  first kerb a little if the car is stable, smooth steering; use the flat
  kerbs mid-corner and on exit, avoid the sausage kerb/gravel, late apex
  for a clean run onto the main straight.

## Common mistakes
Overdriving braking zones into the chicanes, aggressive kerb use through
the Porsche Curves, early throttle at Tertre Rouge and Arnage, losing focus
due to the sheer lap length, and (Hypercar-specific) poor hybrid energy
management on the long straights.
