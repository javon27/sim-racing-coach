# Spa-Francorchamps notes

7.004 km, WEC layout (the corner-map JSON `simcoach/tracks/spa.json`
already covers the 10 officially-numbered corners; this note adds the
wiki's driving detail on top of that existing map -- it does not replace
it, and JSON was not touched per project instructions).

## General
- [LMU] Hypercar can brake significantly later than GT3 into La Source and
  is generally more flat-out through Eau Rouge/Raidillon and Blanchimont in
  race trim once the tyres are stable.
- [LMU] Kerb use throughout is a recurring risk: sausage kerbs and full-lock
  kerb strikes both destabilize the car, especially in Les Fagnes and
  Pouhon.
- [LMU] Setup baseline (both classes): medium-to-low rear wing (stability
  over top speed), start tyre pressures slightly lower (Spa builds heat
  quickly), higher differential preload for stability out of slow corners,
  brake bias slightly forward for La Source/Bus Stop.

## Corner-by-corner (wiki driving detail)
- **La Source:** Very slow right-hand hairpin and the main overtaking spot;
  brake early, don't dive too deep or traction suffers on exit; exit speed
  matters more than minimum corner speed.
- **Eau Rouge/Raidillon:** Flat out on fresh tyres/low fuel; stay tight
  left and keep the car straight over the first kerb, minimal lift in the
  right kink if needed. Hypercar treats the compression as highly sensitive
  to ride height and wants minimal steering at the crest.
- **Les Combes:** Heavy braking after Kemmel Straight, a right-left-right
  sequence; kerbs usable but never at full steering lock; priority is a
  stable exit toward Malmedy (risk of oversteer there).
- **Malmedy:** Slight brake tap, don't overuse the inside kerb, use the
  full width and kerb on exit.
- **Bruxelles:** Long downhill right-hander, patient turn-in -- early
  throttle causes understeer all the way to the exit; constant steering,
  late apex. Hypercar brakes slightly earlier than instinct suggests for
  stability and trail-brakes gently (rear can go unstable downhill).
- **Speaker's Corner:** Light braking after the flag-light on the right,
  use all the kerb but avoid the sausage kerb.
- **Pouhon:** Extremely tyre-intensive; light braking at the flag-light in
  4th, single clean turn-in, avoid mid-corner corrections (big time loss);
  a slightly slower entry with early throttle gives more stability and a
  better exit.
- **Les Fagnes:** Technical right-left combination; clean kerb use, keep
  the car settled -- late apex with trail-braking on the right-hander,
  mid-apex with a slight lift on the left-hander, using the inner kerb to
  help rotate.
- **Campus:** Use all the kerb, brake at the start of the left kerb,
  trail-brake deep to avoid losing rear traction.
- **Stavelot:** Critical for the long flat-out section that follows -- early
  apex, straighten the car early, traction is everything, don't run wide
  into the gravel.
- **Blanchimont:** Flat out for GT3 (fully flat for Hypercar once stable);
  only small corrections, tyre condition determines whether flat-out stays
  possible; watch for slow traffic here in Hypercar.
- **Bus Stop:** Strong overtaking opportunity, brake in a straight line
  (not diagonally), flat kerbs usable, prioritize the exit onto the
  start/finish straight.

## Common mistakes
Over-aggressive turn-in at Pouhon, using kerbs at full steering lock,
throttle too early at Bruxelles and Stavelot, overdriving the tyres in the
opening stint.
