Agentic between-laps coaching

Ask your engineer. Get a straight answer.

Ask “where am I losing time?” or “how do I fix corner-exit oversteer?” Nestor doesn’t dump your whole lap into a prompt — it works out what to look at and pulls only that: one corner, a single lap, your tires, your fuel, or the whole race. Then it answers from those facts.

  • Reaches for the right data itself — a corner, a lap, tire temps, fuel, the race result
  • Never pulls data it doesn’t need — and never guesses a number it wasn’t given
  • Grounded in your real telemetry and current setup, not a generic track guide
How it works

Measured first. Coached second.

Why the advice holds up: the model never touches your raw telemetry, and never invents the numbers. A deterministic pipeline does the measuring — the AI only ever reasons over facts, and fetches exactly the ones each question needs.

1

Capture

Your laps stream in live from LMU’s shared memory at ~50 Hz — brake, throttle, speed, tire temps, fuel and track position — and every lap is stored in full.

Raw telemetry
2

Measure

A deterministic, tested pipeline turns that stream into a ranked read of where a lap gained or lost time, every metric measured against your own best lap, how consistent you're being across the stint, whether the car is understeering, oversteering, locking up or spinning up, how the tires are trending, how much of the grip envelope you're using, and the shape of your own brake/throttle inputs. No AI in this step — just math on your data.

Computed facts
3

Coach

Only those computed facts reach the model. It runs as an agent: for each question it calls the tools it needs — a corner, a lap, tires, fuel, the race result — reads the numbers back, and answers from them.

Agentic loop

What it will never do: see your raw ~50 Hz telemetry, or guess a corner’s number, name or direction. Those are measured from a track map and handed to the model as fact — so its advice always lands on the right turn.

Corners it can trust

It knows every corner — because it measures them.

Corner numbers, names and directions come from the track geometry and a per-track corner map — never guessed by the model. The AI only ever receives a corner’s identity as a fact, so its advice lands on the right turn every time.

  • Direction (left/right) computed from your telemetry
  • Numbered by default — real corner names where the circuit has them
  • An editable track map you build from your own laps
Progress you can see

Watch the improvement, session over session.

Every lap is stored. Nestor tracks your best-lap trend per track and car, your consistency, and how much time you’ve found — so you know the work is paying off, not just feel it.

  • Best-lap trend across every session
  • Personal best, improvement, and consistency at a glance
  • Full history — browse any past session and lap
Live race dashboard

The whole field, on your pit wall.

While you drive, the Pit Wall shows live timing, the gap to every car and where each one sits on track — across every class — so you know who’s coming and how hard to push.

Position — the full field in race order, class gaps and fastest laps.
Relative — the cars nearest you, ahead and behind, with live time gaps.
Why “Nestor”

The oldest, wisest voice in the paddock.

In Homer’s Iliad, Nestor is the eldest of the Greek kings — the counsellor the younger warriors turn to before a fight. He isn’t the fastest or the strongest. He’s the one who has seen the most, and turns hard-won experience into advice you can actually use.

That’s the job. You do the driving; Nestor is the seasoned voice in your ear between stints — and the helm is both: a racing helmet, and a hand on the wheel.
